100% Private Google Sheet & Gmail Integration

How to receive client leads in your Gmail & Google Sheet with ZERO personal data exposed on your website.

CRITICAL SECURITY NOTICE:
Please change your Gmail password immediately. Never paste personal passwords in chat windows or into website code! You do NOT need to give your password to anyone or to your website to receive leads.
Your Privacy is Guaranteed:
Using Google Apps Script, Google's secure servers act as your middleman. Website visitors submit their inquiry, Google records it into your Google Sheet, and emails it to sreejithu@gmail.com. Your email, phone, and password are NEVER visible on the website!
1

Create a New Google Sheet

Open Google Sheets while logged into your Google account:

  • Go to: https://sheets.new
  • Rename the spreadsheet to: Website Client Inquiries
2

Open Apps Script

  • In your Google Sheet top menu, click: Extensions → Apps Script.
  • A code editor tab will open. Delete any code already inside Code.gs.
3

Copy & Paste the Script

Open the file already saved on your Desktop called Google_Apps_Script_Backend.js or copy the code below:

function doPost(e) {
  try {
    var sheet = SpreadsheetApp.getActiveSpreadsheet().getActiveSheet();
    if (sheet.getLastRow() === 0) {
      sheet.appendRow(["Timestamp", "Client Name", "Client Email", "Client Phone", "Service Requested", "Project Scope", "Source"]);
      sheet.getRange(1, 1, 1, 7).setFontWeight("bold").setBackground("#1f2937").setFontColor("#ffffff");
    }
    var data = (e.postData && e.postData.contents) ? JSON.parse(e.postData.contents) : (e.parameter || {});
    var timestamp = new Date();
    sheet.appendRow([timestamp, data.name || "N/A", data.email || "N/A", data.phone || "N/A", data.service || "N/A", data.message || "N/A", data.source || "Website"]);

    MailApp.sendEmail({
      to: "sreejithu@gmail.com",
      subject: "🚀 New Client Lead: " + (data.name || "Website Visitor") + " (" + (data.service || "General") + ")",
      body: "Hello Sreejith,\n\nYou have received a new client inquiry on your website!\n\n" +
            "• Name: " + data.name + "\n" +
            "• Email: " + data.email + "\n" +
            "• Phone: " + data.phone + "\n" +
            "• Service: " + data.service + "\n" +
            "• Requirements:\n" + data.message + "\n\n" +
            "Recorded in your Google Sheet automatically."
    });

    return ContentService.createTextOutput(JSON.stringify({ status: "success" })).setMimeType(ContentService.MimeType.JSON);
  } catch (error) {
    return ContentService.createTextOutput(JSON.stringify({ status: "error", error: error.toString() })).setMimeType(ContentService.MimeType.JSON);
  }
}

Click the Save (disk) icon at the top of Apps Script.

4

Deploy as Web App

  • Click the blue Deploy button (top right) → New deployment.
  • Click the gear icon next to Select type → choose Web app.
  • Set Execute as to: Me.
  • Set Who has access to: Anyone (this allows your website to securely send the form).
  • Click Deploy → Click Authorize access → Choose your Google account → Click Allow.
  • Copy the Web app URL (it starts with https://script.google.com/macros/s/...).
5

Connect to Home.html

Open Home.html in Notepad or any editor, scroll to line ~2160, and paste your URL into:

this.googleAppsScriptEndpoint = "YOUR_WEB_APP_URL_HERE";

That's it! Now whenever someone fills out the Contact Form or uses the WhatsApp chat screen on your website:

  • The client's details will appear in your Google Sheet immediately.
  • You will get an email notification in your Gmail with all their project details.
  • No visitor will ever see your personal email, phone number, or credentials!
