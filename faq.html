Frequently Asked Questions
Clear Answers • Honest Working Terms

Everything you need to know about how I work, payment terms, how demos are conducted, intellectual property rights, and direct communication.

What are your payment terms? Do you require an advance payment?

No advance or initial payment is required. I work strictly on a demo-first, satisfaction-guaranteed basis:

  • Step 1: You share the details of the task or software you need, and we sign a mutual NDA upfront.
  • Step 2: I build the working solution myself (process automation bot, Android app, or web tool).
  • Step 3: I show you a live, interactive demonstration where you can test and review how it works.
  • Step 4: If you are completely satisfied with my work, only then does the billing process happen.
  • Step 5: After payment, the complete source code, files, and setup instructions are handed over to you.

If you do not like the work, you can cancel at any time without any cost or obligation.

What happens if I don't like the work or decide to cancel?

You can cancel immediately at any time with zero cost and zero penalties. Since no advance or deposit was taken, you take on zero financial risk. I believe in demonstrating real value before asking for payment.

How much do you charge? How is pricing decided?

My primary focus is helping small company owners and growing businesses that need practical automation without heavy corporate expenses. Since I work independently without company overhead or middleman fees, my pricing is fair, budget-friendly, and discussed directly with you based on your exact requirements. We agree on the cost upfront before I begin building.

How do we communicate during the project?

We communicate directly 1-on-1 via WhatsApp, phone calls, email, and live screen-sharing (Google Meet or Zoom) whenever needed. You have direct access to me for quick questions, status updates, and live walkthroughs.

Who owns the source code and software once finished?

You own 100% of the code, scripts, and intellectual property. Once the project is approved and settled, the full source code, database setup, and all project files are unconditionally transferred to you. You are free to modify, host, or deploy it however you like.

Do you sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)?

Yes, absolutely. Before you share any sensitive business workflows, spreadsheets, database credentials, or customer information, I sign a mutual NDA to ensure your proprietary business data remains 100% confidential.

What technologies do you use for development and business tools?

We choose the most reliable and efficient technologies suited to your exact project requirements. Rather than forcing a single rigid stack, we evaluate your website, business environment, and practical needs during your appointment to build the best tools for the job.

Do you provide support after the software is delivered?

Yes. Every project includes a complimentary 90-day bug warranty. If any issue arises from the code I wrote during this period, I resolve it directly at zero additional charge. For ongoing needs, I also offer affordable monthly support.
